About Our Community

A space to connect, celebrate, and protect the cultures of Queensland's First Nations peoples.

Who We Are

The Queensland Nations Community Hub is a non-profit platform designed to support connection, information sharing, and cultural preservation across diverse Indigenous communities in Queensland.

For tens of thousands of years, the Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples of Queensland have maintained deep and enduring connections to Country. Their languages, stories, ceremonies, and knowledge systems represent one of the world's oldest living cultures. This website is a space to honour those connections — to share community news, celebrate cultural events, and hear the voices of community members across generations.

Our Principles

Our platform is built on the values that guide everything we share:

  • Respect — for the people, lands, and knowledge behind every story.
  • Inclusivity — a welcoming space for community members and allies alike.
  • Authenticity — content that is community-led and meaningfully represented.

How We Protect Culture

Cultural knowledge belongs to the people and communities it comes from. We respect Indigenous Cultural and Intellectual Property (ICIP): material should only be shared with the free, prior and informed consent of the people whose story it is.

Protecting culture does not always mean making everything public. Sometimes the most respectful choice is to preserve something while keeping it within a family or community. We aim to honour that throughout this platform.

Acknowledgement of Country

We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the lands on which we operate, and pay our respects to Elders past, present, and emerging. We recognise their continuing connection to land, waters, and community.
