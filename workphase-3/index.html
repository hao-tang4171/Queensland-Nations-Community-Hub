Welcome to Country

A space to share, celebrate, and protect culture

Connecting the communities of Queensland's First Nations peoples through stories, events, and shared knowledge.

The Queensland Nations Community Hub is a non-profit platform for connection, information sharing, and cultural preservation across diverse Indigenous communities in Queensland.

It is a space to honour deep and enduring connections to Country — to share community news, celebrate cultural events, and hear voices across generations.

Caring for Cultural Knowledge

A few principles that guide how this platform treats cultural material.

FPIC

Free, Prior and Informed Consent — material is only shared when the person whose story it is has agreed, knowing where it will go.

ICIP

Indigenous Cultural and Intellectual Property — cultural knowledge belongs to the communities it comes from.

Protect ≠ Publish

Protecting culture does not always mean making it public. Sometimes the respectful choice is to preserve it privately.
